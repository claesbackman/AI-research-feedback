How this site was built

The pages are generated from the paper's LaTeX source with pandoc. Figures, tables and equations keep the numbers they have in the PDF, footnotes become inline notes, and citations link to the reference list. The math is rendered with KaTeX. The site is static and needs no server. Built on @@BUILD_DATE@@.
